<scope>
Consider only SQL/NoSQL injection, OS command injection, LFI/RFI, path traversal, SSTI, and unsafe deserialization observations. Ignore every other class.

<class_boundary>

Group observations only when the same request, input, sink, defense, and single injection proof would settle all of them with one verdict. Shared CWE, file, line, impact, or remediation is not enough.
</class_boundary>
